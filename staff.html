Meet the team

Dental Staff

All professionals at Jay K Doshi Dental Assoc., PA maintain the highest levels of accreditation and pursue ongoing education to stay abreast of the latest trends in dentistry. Read on to learn more about our team's experience and training.

Practice Owner

Jay Doshi, DDS

Dr. Jay Doshi, DDS, is the practice owner of Family Dental Care in Spring, Texas. He is recognized as one of the finest in Texas when it comes to the care for an entire family's dental needs. Dr. Doshi has positively transformed the smiles of thousands of patients throughout his career.

Dr. Doshi graduated from The University of Texas–Houston dental branch in 2000.

Today, Dr. Doshi performs all phases of dentistry. These include teeth whitening, implant-supported crowns, bridges, and full smile makeovers. He sees patients from 6 months old to our older geriatric patients and everything in between.

Dr. Doshi is educated in the latest techniques, allowing us to offer our patients the best dental treatments available, including Invisalign and any phase of cosmetic dentistry.

Our customer-service-focused staff takes pride in making every visit a positive and relaxing experience for patients of all ages.

When Dr. Doshi is not busy creating beautiful smiles, he can be found exploring all of what Houston has to offer. He is also a proud Houston sports fan — ask him about it at your next visit!
