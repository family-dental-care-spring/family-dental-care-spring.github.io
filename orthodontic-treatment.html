Orthodontics

Orthodontic Treatment

Crowded, crooked, or misaligned teeth aren't just a cosmetic concern — they can make brushing and flossing harder, wear teeth unevenly, and put extra strain on the jaw joints. Orthodontic treatment gradually guides teeth into their correct position, improving both the health and the appearance of your bite.

What orthodontic treatment can correct

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • An overbite, underbite, or crossbite
  • Teeth that have shifted after tooth loss or years of wear
  • Jaw alignment issues that contribute to uneven bite pressure

Treatment options

Traditional braces. Metal or ceramic brackets are bonded to each tooth and connected with an archwire, which is adjusted at regular visits to gradually move teeth into alignment. Braces remain one of the most effective options, especially for more complex bite corrections.

Invisalign. A series of clear, custom-fitted aligners gradually shift teeth into place. Each set is worn for about one to two weeks before moving to the next in the series. Aligners are removable for eating and brushing, and are far less noticeable than traditional braces — a popular choice for image-conscious teens and adults.

Your dentist will evaluate your bite, the position of your teeth, and your goals to recommend the option that will give you the most effective and comfortable results.

What to expect

  • An initial exam, including X-rays or a digital scan, to evaluate your bite and plan your treatment.
  • Placement of braces or delivery of your first set of aligners.
  • Regular follow-up visits — to adjust braces or check aligner progress — typically every four to eight weeks.
  • A retainer, worn after treatment is complete, to keep your teeth from shifting back out of alignment.

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of the case, typically ranging from several months to a couple of years. Orthodontic treatment isn't just for kids and teens — many adults choose to straighten their smile later in life, and it's never too late to ask. Talk to Dr. Doshi about whether braces or Invisalign are the right fit for your smile.
