Appointments

We know you have many choices when choosing a dentist in Spring, TX, so we've made requesting an appointment a simple process via our website. If, for any reason, you cannot keep a scheduled appointment, or will be delayed, please call us as soon as possible.

Insurance and Billing

We accept most insurance plans, including PPOs, Medicaid, CHIP, Medicare, DMOs, and discount plans — contact our office to verify acceptance of your specific plan. We will be happy to file your insurance claims for you. We accept checks, cash, or credit cards, and also offer a flexible payment plan. Please see our financial coordinator for details.

Payment Options

We accept checks, cash, or credit cards. We also offer a flexible payment plan — please see our financial coordinator for details.

Insurance Options

The insurance industry changes often, and our team does our best to retrieve accurate information on your behalf. We are happy to file insurance for your reimbursement as long as you understand that insurance can only estimate, not guarantee, payment. All elected treatment remains your financial responsibility, but we'll do everything we can to help you get the full benefit of your coverage.

Financing Options

CareCredit is here to help you pay for treatments and procedures your insurance doesn't cover. We offer no-interest financing or low minimum monthly payment options, so you can get the care you want, when you want it.

CareCredit — with a simple three-step application and instant approval process, you're free to use CareCredit for the dental services you choose. Visit carecredit.com to learn more or apply online.
